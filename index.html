01 / INTRODUCING MEOWSLIM

See yourself in the little details.

Meowslim is an avatar maker built around representation and modesty. Start with a name or choose a style, then bring together a character that feels familiar, expressive and recognisably you.

Like DiceBear, the underlying product uses a seed to create a repeatable avatar. Unlike a generic character collection, Meowslim puts Muslim dress and identity at the heart of its designs.
