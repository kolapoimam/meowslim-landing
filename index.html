04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few little
questions.

What is Meowslim? +

Meowslim is an upcoming avatar creator designed to celebrate Muslim identity through playful, customizable illustrated characters.

Can I create an avatar right now? +

Not just yet. This is our first landing page. We're working on the artwork and interactive avatar maker next.

Will there be hijabs, niqabs, burqas, and turbans? +

Yes! Those are part of the planned collection, alongside khimars, kufis, caps, beards, expressions, and more.

Will Meowslim be free? +

Our goal is a free, accessible avatar maker. We'll share final details as the project takes shape.

When will it launch? +

We haven't announced a date. Follow the GitHub project to see progress as we build it.
